# V2.1 — Platform Owner Dashboard

## Goal
Build a separate owner-only area for the Sellora platform operator. Tenant admins must not see or call platform administration features.

## Overview
- KPI cards: total businesses, active subscriptions, trials, overdue/locked accounts, monthly recurring revenue, yearly subscribers.
- Recent subscription and payment events.
- Business directory with server-side search/filtering by business name, owner email, plan, billing interval, and status.
- Business detail page with owner/contact details, subscription dates, payment history, and audit history.
- Never expose passwords, payment secrets, or sensitive credentials.

## Subscription and pricing controls
- Launch prices: ₹249/month and ₹2,490/year; yearly term is 12 months for the price of 10.
- View current plan, next due date, last successful payment, and status.
- Authorized owner can trial, suspend/reactivate, or correct subscription state with a reason and audit event. Manual actions must never fabricate a payment.
- Price changes apply to new subscriptions by default; existing subscriptions retain their agreed price unless an explicit migration is approved.
- INR and tax treatment must be clear before checkout.

## Security
- Every platform endpoint checks authenticated role equals `PLATFORM_OWNER` on the server.
- Use existing owner bootstrap configuration and a trusted role-promotion process for a verified existing account; never silently promote a regular admin.
- Pagination, input validation, audit logging, and explicit loading/empty/error states are required.

## Acceptance tests
- Unauthenticated calls return 401; tenant admins receive 403 for every platform endpoint.
- Owner can search businesses and inspect subscription details.
- Suspension/reactivation is audited and affects protected access checks.
- No secrets or payment credentials appear in API responses.
