# V2.1 — Platform Owner Dashboard

## Goal
Create a separate owner-only area for the Sellora platform operator. Ordinary tenant admins must not see or call platform administration features.

## Navigation and overview
- Dedicated Platform Owner navigation/route, shown only when `/auth/me` confirms `isPlatformOwner`.
- KPI cards: total businesses, active subscriptions, trials, overdue/locked accounts, monthly recurring revenue, and yearly subscribers.
- Recent subscription events and recent payment events.

## Business directory
- Search/filter by business name, owner email, plan, billing interval, and subscription status.
- Detail view includes business identity, owner/contact, created date, subscription state, period dates, payment history, and audit history.
- Do not expose tenant passwords, payment secrets, or sensitive credentials.

## Subscription controls
- View current plan and billing interval.
- Activate a trial, assign monthly/yearly billing, suspend/reactivate access, and record a renewal only through audited, authorized operations.
- Show next due date, amount due, last successful payment, and reason/status.
- Manual overrides require a reason and an audit event. They must not fabricate a successful payment.

## Pricing controls
- Launch prices: ₹249/month and ₹2,490/year.
- Yearly term is 12 months for the price of 10.
- Price changes apply to new subscriptions by default; existing subscriptions keep their agreed price unless an explicit migration is approved.
- Currency INR. Clearly display tax treatment at checkout.

## API/security
- Every platform endpoint checks authenticated user role equals `PLATFORM_OWNER` on the server.
- Owner bootstrap uses the existing `PLATFORM_OWNER_EMAIL` and `PLATFORM_OWNER_PASSWORD` configuration and a trusted role-promotion procedure for an existing verified account; never silently promote regular admins.
- Add pagination, server-side search/filtering, validation, and audit logging.
- No client-side-only authorization.

## Acceptance tests
- Tenant admin receives 403 for every platform endpoint.
- Unauthenticated requests receive 401.
- Owner can search businesses and view subscription details.
- Manual suspension/reactivation is audited and affects protected access checks.
- UI states loading, empty, error, and success correctly.
