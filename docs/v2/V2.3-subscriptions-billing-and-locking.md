# V2.3 — Subscription Billing, Due Dates, and Access Locking

## Pricing
- Monthly: ₹249.
- Yearly: ₹2,490 for 12 months, equivalent to paying for 10 months.
- Same feature set for both intervals at launch.
- Clearly show taxes and exact payable amount before checkout.

## Billing and payment confirmation
- Use Sellora platform billing credentials, separate from tenant-owned Razorpay credentials used for their own customer sales.
- Investigate provider-supported recurring UPI AutoPay/mandates where eligible; also support secure hosted checkout for manual renewal where required. Do not assume AutoPay is available for every user or method.
- Create payment attempts server-side and mark paid only after verified provider webhook/API confirmation.
- Webhook processing must be idempotent and reconcile duplicate/delayed events.
- Show payment receipts/history and pending/failed states.

## Due date and lock behavior
- Persist interval, period start/end, due date, amount, provider IDs, payment status, and timestamps.
- Use Asia/Kolkata for billing date decisions at launch.
- Example: due on the 11th means access remains active through the 11th. If payment is not verified by the end of that day, lock workspace and public catalogue access from the 12th.
- Enforce locks in backend APIs and public catalogue routes, not only in the UI.
- Preserve tenant data while locked and show an unavailable/renewal page instead of exposing catalogue content.
- Verified payment extends the term and restores access automatically; pending, failed, or unverified payments do not unlock.

## Customer and owner experience
- Customer sees plan, price, period, next due date, status, history, and secure Pay/Renew action.
- Owner dashboard highlights overdue/locked accounts and verified payment events.
- Notifications are added only when a channel is configured.
- Define cancellation, refunds, disputes, and provider-outage behavior.

## Acceptance tests
- Verified payment extends exactly once; duplicate webhooks do not double-extend.
- An unpaid subscription due on the 11th locks from the 12th in Asia/Kolkata.
- Pending/failed payment does not unlock.
- Verified post-expiry payment restores dashboard and public catalogue.
- Direct API calls and public URLs cannot bypass subscription enforcement.
- Tenant storefront payment settings are not changed by platform billing.
