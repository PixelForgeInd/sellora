# V2.3 — Subscription Billing, Due Dates, and Access Locking

## Goal
Provide a reliable platform subscription lifecycle for every tenant, with a single launch plan and two billing intervals.

## Pricing
- Monthly: ₹249.
- Yearly: ₹2,490 for 12 months, equivalent to paying for 10 months.
- Same features for monthly and yearly subscribers at launch.
- Display taxes and the exact amount payable before checkout.

## Checkout and payment confirmation
- Use Sellora's platform billing provider configuration, kept separate from tenant-owned Razorpay credentials used to collect their own customer payments.
- Investigate and implement the provider-supported recurring mandate/UPI AutoPay flow where eligible; also provide a secure hosted payment checkout for supported one-time/manual renewals.
- Do not assume UPI AutoPay is enabled for every customer or payment method.
- Create/associate a subscription and payment attempt server-side.
- Mark paid only after a verified provider webhook/API confirmation; make webhook processing idempotent and reconcile delayed/duplicate events.
- Provide payment receipts/history and clear pending/failed states.

## Dates and expiry
- Persist billing interval, period start/end, due date, amount, provider identifiers, payment status, and timestamps.
- Use Asia/Kolkata for launch billing dates.
- Example: if payment is due on the 11th, keep access available through the 11th. If payment remains unpaid at the end of the 11th, lock access from the 12th.
- The locked state applies to tenant dashboard APIs and public catalogue pages, not just the UI.
- Preserve data while locked. Public catalogue visitors should see a clear unavailable/temporarily inactive page, not private tenant data.
- On verified payment success, extend the subscription period and restore the catalogue and workspace automatically.
- Pending, failed, cancelled, or unverified payments must not unlock the account.

## Owner and customer experience
- Customer sees plan, price, billing period, next due date, payment status, payment history, and a Pay/Renew action.
- Owner dashboard sees overdue/locked accounts and verified payment events.
- Send due-date and payment-failure notifications only after a notification channel is configured.
- Cancellation, refunds, disputes, and payment-provider outages need explicit states and documented behavior.

## Acceptance tests
- Successful payment activates/extends access exactly once.
- Duplicate webhooks do not double-extend a term.
- Unpaid account due on the 11th is locked from the 12th in Asia/Kolkata.
- Payment pending/failed does not unlock.
- Verified payment after lock restores workspace and public catalogue.
- Direct API calls and public URLs cannot bypass lock state.
- Tenant subscription billing does not alter tenant storefront Razorpay settings.
